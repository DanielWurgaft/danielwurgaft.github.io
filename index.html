I'm a PhD student at Stanford University, advised by Noah Goodman. I study in-context learning, representational geometry, and reasoning in both language models and humans, drawing on normative frameworks to explain why these systems behave and represent information as they do. I've also worked on using LLMs to scale and automate scientific discovery in cognitive science.

Previously, I spent time as a research fellow at Goodfire AI, and completed my undergrad at the University of Toronto in psychology and computer science.
